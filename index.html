How splits are measured. Each split runs between two timeline events from the match: spawn → enter Nether → find bastion → find fortress → blind travel → follow eye (stronghold) → enter End → finish. A split only counts when both ends happened, so forfeits still contribute the splits they reached. Terrain to Bastion, Bastion and Fortress splits only count bastion-first runs; fortress-first runs are excluded from those three. A reset restarts the seed, so post-reset attempts are left out unless you include them.

How % is calculated. Each split's Top / Bottom % compares your average time for that split with every time for the same split in the 350 most recent ranked matches of season 12 (all players, collected 8 October 2026). "Top 10%" means your average is faster than 90% of those runs. Later splits are compared against fewer runs, because many games end before reaching them. Netherite = top 5%, Diamond = top 20%, Emerald = top 40%, Gold = top 60%, Iron = bottom 40%, Coal = bottom 20%. With a seed-type filter on, you are compared against runs on the same seed type when the sample has at least 30 of them.

How the rank distribution is calculated. This chart uses Elo ranks (Coal I to Netherite), which are different from the split tiers above. There is no list of every ranked player, so it is built from a sample: match pages spread evenly across the season. Every player in those matches who has finished placements is counted once, at their current Elo. Regular players are almost all included, but some who played very few games may be missing, so the percentages are an estimate. Elo ranges: Coal I 0–399, Coal II 400–499, Coal III 500–599, then 100 Elo per division up to Emerald III (1400–1499); Diamond I 1500–1649, Diamond II 1650–1799, Diamond III 1800–1999, Netherite 2000+.

Data from the MCSR Ranked API. Match details are cached in your browser, so repeat searches are fast.
