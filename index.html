How splits are measured. Each split runs between two timeline events from the match: spawn → enter Nether → find bastion → find fortress → blind travel → follow eye (stronghold) → enter End → finish. A split only counts when both ends happened, so forfeits still contribute the splits they reached. Terrain to Bastion, Bastion and Fortress splits only count bastion-first runs; fortress-first runs are excluded from those three. A reset restarts the seed, so post-reset attempts are left out unless you include them.

How ranks work. Your average for each split is placed among split times from a sample of recent ranked matches by all players. Netherite = top 5%, Diamond = top 20%, Emerald = top 40%, Gold = top 60%, Iron = bottom 40%, Coal = bottom 20%. With a seed-type filter on, you are compared against runs on the same seed type when the sample has at least 30 of them.

Data from the MCSR Ranked API. Match details are cached in your browser, so repeat searches are fast.
